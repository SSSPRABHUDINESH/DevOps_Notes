**2. There are two people in your team who are unable to write Terraform code for a specific part and are struggling to get into it. What will you try to do to meet the project deadlines?**

**Answer:**
"When team members are struggling with a specific technical task like Terraform, my priority is a balanced approach: protecting project timelines while supporting and upskilling the team.

Here is the step-by-step approach I would follow:

1. **Assess the Bottleneck Immediately:**
I would connect with the two team members quickly to understand where they are stuck—whether it's understanding state management, modular design, provider configurations, or syntax.
2. **Unblock and Pair Program:**
Instead of taking over the task completely (which removes a learning opportunity) or leaving them to struggle further, I would carve out time for a quick 1-on-1 or pair-programming session. Working through the roadblock together helps them grasp the concept and gets the code moving forward.
3. **Re-evaluate and Redistribute (If Needed):**
If the timeline is extremely tight and the task is complex, I would split the deliverable:
* Have them handle the parts they are comfortable with or can learn quickly.
* Take on or reassign the most critical/complex block to a senior member to ensure we meet the deadline.


4. **Deliver and Document:**
Once the code is successfully written and reviewed, I would ensure we document the solution or create reusable Terraform modules so that the team won't face the same hurdle in future phases of the project."
